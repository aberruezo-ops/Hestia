Jerez Horse Fair, first week of May

Fair · Andalucía · May 2026

Home › News & Blog › May 2026 › Jerez Horse Fair, first week of May

The most flamenco of all Andalusian fairs. Carriages, flounced dresses, sherry and farruca until dawn. Jerez is 3 hours from Vera Playa: if your dates overlap and you have a car, it is unmissable.

The month in numbers. In May, highs hover around 24°C and lows around 15°C (indicative historical average for the Almería coast).

Written by Alex and Fran, who have run the three Hestía apartments in Vera Playa since 2016.
