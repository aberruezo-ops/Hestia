02

Trail

Here are 10. The guest guide has 18, with a tip for each.

Vera Playa → Garrucha (paseo litoral)

Flat walk along the shore from Vera Playa to Garrucha's fishing harbour. No incline, buggy or elderly-friendly. At the harbour, fried-fish restaurants serve the area's best catch.

Must-seeView on map
a short walkfrom home

Vera Playa → Villaricos (ruinas romanas)

Walk north along the beach and low cliffs to Villaricos, where remains of a 3rd-century BC Phoenician and Roman colony stand. Ends at the Archaeological Park with views over the Almanzora delta.

View on map
1.3 kmfrom home

Vera Playa → Vera pueblo (por el barranquillo)

A roughly 40-minute walk inland, climbing gently along the old road and rural track to Vera's historic centre. Ends at the 16th-century fortress-cathedral, built to withstand Berber raids.

View on map
3.2 kmfrom home

Paseo costero Mojácar Playa – Garrucha

An easy walk (no technical difficulty) along Mojácar Playa's Paseo del Mediterráneo to Garrucha's fishing harbour. No incline, great with kids. Arriving at the harbour with the fishing boats and fried-fish spots makes it a complete plan.

View on map
5.8 kmfrom home

Sendero La Mena – Macenas (PR-A 96)

Linear coastal route between the cliffs of Mojácar and Macenas beach. Passes the 16th-century Torre del Pirulico watchtower and several viewpoints.

Must-seeView on map
14 kmfrom home

Las Negras → Cala de San Pedro

Coastal trail along cliffs to a remote cove with a 15th-century Nasrid castle and crystal-clear water. On arrival you'll find a small self-sufficient community selling coffee and fruit. No road access.

Must-seeView on map
43 kmfrom home

San José → Los Genoveses → Mónsul (PR-A 47)

The Natural Park's most spectacular coastal route. Passes through the open bay of Los Genoveses and ends at Mónsul, with its black volcanic rocks and dune-sculpting waves. UNESCO World Heritage.

Must-seeView on map
58 kmfrom home

Sendero de Las Cámaras (Sierra Espuña, Murcia)

Murcia's largest natural area: century-old reforested pine woods and the Las Cámaras via ferrata and trail up to the Morrón de Totana, with Spanish ibex sightings almost guaranteed.

Must-seeView on map
73 kmfrom home

Barrancos de Gebas (Alhama de Murcia)

A landscape of gullies and badlands shaped by the erosion of marl and clay, with narrow canyons and formations that look like another planet. Quite unlike anything on the coast.

Must-seeView on map
75 kmfrom home

Cerrada de Utrero (Sierra de Castril, Granada)

Karst canyon at the northern tip of Granada province, with the Castril river rising between limestone walls, waterfalls and wooden walkways over the water. Natural Park.

Must-seeView on map

Outdoor karting track in Garrucha. Separate adult and children's karts. Distance: ~10 min by car from Vera Playa.

Lunar Cable Park (wakeboard · ski náutico)

Cable ski on the former rowing canal of the 2005 Mediterranean Games, next to the Cuevas del Almanzora reservoir. Reopened in 2019 as Lunar Cable Park. No boat, no wake, the cable pulls you along. Distance: ~20 km / 24 min from Vera Playa by local road via Vera and Cuevas del Almanzora.
