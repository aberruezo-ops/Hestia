Hestía Thalassa · Apartment in Vera Playa, Almería

Hestía Thalassa is the penthouse of the Al Andalus Thalassa complex, in Vera Playa (Almería). From its 18 m² panoramic terrace you see the Mediterranean on one side and the Salar de los Canos on the other.

The complex has two outdoor pools (north and south), gardens, parks and padel and tennis courts, and a shared spa with heated pool, jacuzzi, sauna, foot bath, gym and relaxation area (the spa opens in autumn, winter and spring; the gym all year).

Two bedrooms and space for 6 guests plus a baby. Two en-suite bathrooms with hydromassage columns and a full kitchen with a high-end dishwasher and washing machine. Air conditioning in the living room and bedrooms, fibre WiFi and a Nespresso machine.

It is the apartment with the most streaming platforms: SkyShowtime, HBO Max, Prime Video, Movistar Plus and Disney+, all active.

The beach is 1.5 kilometres away. Pets are welcome on prior request, with a moderate supplement per night. There is a minimum stay, a little higher in peak season.

Tourist licence VFT/AL/05535.
