Hestía for companies and teams · Vera Playa, Almería

Accommodation for companies, teams and business travel in Vera Playa (Almería).

All three apartments have 2 bedrooms and space for 6 people, fibre WiFi, a full kitchen and room to work, which makes them practical for relocated teams, film shoots, building works or seasonal projects in the Almería Levante.

We work with invoices, a contract and terms agreed in writing. For long stays, from one month onwards, we apply a fixed monthly rate.

The area concentrates intensive agriculture, energy and construction, as well as film shoots in the Tabernas Desert, about 30 minutes by car.

Direct management with Alex and Fran, no intermediaries, with a reply in under two hours. Tell us your dates, number of people and needs and we will prepare a proposal.
