Hestía Your Home · Your home in Vera Playa, Almería

Three holiday apartments in Vera Playa (Almería), run directly by Alex and Fran since 2016, with no intermediaries and no platform commissions.

The three apartments

Hestía Mar is the closest to the beach, 300 metres away, with a 20 m² corner terrace, palm trees and partial sea views. Hestía Thalassa is the penthouse, with panoramic views over the Mediterranean and the Salar de los Canos, a shared spa, pools and padel and tennis courts in the complex. Hestía Salinas is the largest, with two terraces and the Salinas de Puerto Rey bird reserve a short walk away.

All three have 2 bedrooms, space for 6 guests plus a baby, a pool, a full kitchen, air conditioning, fibre WiFi and a Smart TV with streaming platforms included. Pets are welcome in all three, on prior request and with a supplement.

The area: Vera Playa and the Almería Levante

Vera Playa is the base for exploring the Almería Levante: Mojácar, Garrucha, the Cabo de Gata Natural Park, the Tabernas Desert and the beaches of Villaricos and Palomares.
