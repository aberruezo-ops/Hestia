Long stays in Vera Playa · Remote work and winter

Stays of a month or more in Vera Playa (Almería), designed for remote work, winter seasons or long stays on the Almería coast.

Available from September to June. We do not offer long stays in July and August.

The rate is monthly and fixed, and varies by season. Christmas (23 December to 6 January) and Easter carry a supplement. Check the current price on the website.

It includes fibre WiFi, a full kitchen, washing machine, heating and cooling air conditioning, and access to the shared areas of each complex: a pool in all three and, at Thalassa, a shared spa with sauna and gym.

Vera Playa has services all year, a mild winter climate and good connections to Almería, Murcia and the airports in the area, which makes it comfortable for spending the cold season.

Pets are welcome on prior request. Tell us your dates and we confirm availability and a fixed price.
