Hestía Mar · Apartment in Vera Playa, Almería

Hestía Mar is a holiday apartment in Vera Playa (Almería), 300 metres from the beach, the closest to the sea of the three Hestías. You can walk there, no car needed.

Its 20 m² corner terrace looks towards the Mediterranean among the palm trees and is where the day begins. The complex garden has a shared pool, jacuzzi and children's playground.

Two bedrooms and space for 6 guests plus a baby, with a cot and high chair on request. Two bathrooms: one with a hydrotherapy column and chromotherapy mirror, the other with a shower. Full kitchen with dishwasher and washing machine, ducted air conditioning, fibre WiFi and covered garage.

A 55-inch Smart TV with SkyShowtime, HBO Max, Prime Video and Disney+ active from the first minute of your stay.

Pets are welcome on prior request, with a moderate supplement per night. There is a minimum stay, a little higher in peak season, and an additional guest carries a small supplement.

Tourist licence VFT/AL/01580.
