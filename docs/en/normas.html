House rules · Hestía Your Home

The rules for using the three Hestía apartments in Vera Playa, published before you book so there are no surprises. They come from the contract you sign and exist so that you, the next guests and the neighbours can all enjoy the place.

Check-in from 15:00 and check-out before 11:00. We need that margin to get the apartment ready for the next arrival. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability. Arrival is self check-in by default; in-person arrival is free between 15:00 and 19:00 and, outside those hours, carries a cost and is subject to availability.

Hestía is only for the people on the booking, never for third parties. All overnight guests go on the contract and on the traveller register, as Spanish regulations require.

No smoking inside the apartment, in any room. Towels stay inside Hestía: for the pool and the beach, everyone brings their own. Laundry is hung only on the clothes rack, never on railings or on the terrace.

Rest is respected, yours and the neighbours', avoiding noise, music or commotion at unsociable hours. When you go out, tidy the terrace (cushions, awning and plants), especially if it is windy or bad weather is forecast.

In shared areas the rules of each complex apply, especially pool hours. Naturism and topless sunbathing are not allowed in any area of the complex. Drive slowly inside the community: there are children, pets and pedestrians.

Relieving oneself in the pool and shared areas is strictly forbidden. Babies must always swim in a swim nappy. Minors remain the responsibility of their parents or guardians.

Pets are welcome in all three apartments, always on prior request and with a moderate supplement per night, with a cap per stay.

Look after the equipment and furniture: we do an inventory after the stay. And look after the surroundings, without wasting electricity or water and without leaving the air conditioning on with the windows open or when you go out.

Anything can break or fail. What we guarantee is that we will be on hand and do everything we can to solve it as soon as possible.
